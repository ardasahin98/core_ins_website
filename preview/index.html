End to end

One team from the plan to the 3am alarm

Most instrumentation problems happen at the handover between suppliers — the sensor is fine, the installation is fine, and nobody owns the number. We do all of it, so there is one phone call when something moves.

Our services Browse instruments
01

Monitoring design

We turn a design intent or a permit condition into an instrumentation plan: what to measure, where, at what frequency, and what the trigger levels should be.

02

Supply & rental

Instruments, dataloggers, cable and enclosures from stock, with calibration certificates. Short-term rental for construction-phase monitoring.

03

Installation & commissioning

Field crews install, splice, commission and prove the system, then hand over baseline readings you can defend.

04

Managed monitoring

We run the system for the life of the project: automated collection, validation, alarms and reporting, with someone on the end of the phone at 3am.

05

Maintenance & calibration

Planned service visits, recalibration and fault response, so the record stays continuous.
